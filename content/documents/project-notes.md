# Project notes (SAMPLE CONTENT)

## Pricing-tier redesign — Ledgerly, 2023
- Problem: churn among small teams (2–10 seats) was the highest of any segment, at 6.1% per month.
- What Alex did: analysed 18 months of product usage and billing data. Found that most churn came from teams that outgrew the free plan's limits but saw the paid plan as too big a jump.
- Proposal: a new mid-tier plan between Free and Business. Alex ran a pricing survey with 400 customers and a 6-week price test.
- Alex's role: led the analysis end to end and presented the recommendation to the executive team. Worked with one product manager and one designer.
- Result: small-team churn fell from 6.1% to 4.3% per month within two quarters. The mid-tier now makes up about 18% of new paid sign-ups.

## Experimentation platform — Northwind SaaS, 2020–2021
- Problem: experiments were analysed by hand in spreadsheets, with inconsistent statistics and frequent mistakes.
- What Alex did: built a standard pipeline (SQL + Python) that assigns users, tracks metrics and produces a results report for every test.
- Result: time to analyse a test went from about 3 days to under 2 hours. More than 60 tests ran on it during Alex's time there.
